Design System · Brand Reference

NCET — Color Palette

The working color system of the NCET website, extracted from the live frontend CSS. A brand-led palette anchored on Navy, Orange and Blue, backed by a neutral slate/gray scale and soft brand tints.
Primary Navy #0E2455 · 649 uses Accent Orange #F6872A · 461 uses Accent Blue #3270FC · 149 uses

1 Brand / Primary Colors

The core identity. Navy is the foundation (backgrounds, headings, nav, footer); Orange is the signature accent used for highlights, CTAs and title emphasis; Blue is the tertiary accent for links and info.
